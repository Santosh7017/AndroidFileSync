Features

Everything You Need to Manage Your Phone

AndroidFileSync gives you full control over your Android device — file management, app control, and seamless connectivity.

File Browser

Navigate your phone storage like macOS Finder. Search, sort by name/size/type, and preview files instantly.

Drag & Drop

Drag files from your Mac straight to your phone. The most natural way to transfer files — just like between Finder windows.

USB & WiFi

Connect with a USB cable for maximum speed, or go wireless over your home network. Auto-discovery finds your phone instantly.

App Manager

Install APKs, uninstall apps, disable system bloatware, force-stop misbehaving apps, and backup APKs — all from your Mac.

File Preview

Double-click any file to preview images, videos, PDFs, and documents without downloading them first. Powered by macOS Quick Look.

Parallel Transfers

Upload and download multiple files simultaneously with live progress tracking. Collapsible transfer panel for clutter-free workflow.

Trash & Restore

Soft-delete files with a dedicated Trash folder on your device. Restore accidentally deleted files with one click — just like macOS.

SD Card Support

Browse and manage files on your external SD card. Full read/write access, just like internal storage.

Copy, Cut & Paste

Clipboard operations across folders on your device. Move files between directories without downloading to your Mac first.

Batch Operations

Rename multiple files, change extensions in bulk, or delete several items at once. Save time with batch actions.

Duplicate Detection

Automatically detects duplicate files during uploads. Choose to skip or replace — never accidentally overwrite important data.

Safe by Default

Confirmation dialogs before every destructive action. Context-aware buttons — you always know what will happen next.

How It Works

Set Up in Under 2 Minutes

Get connected and start transferring files faster than any other Android file manager for Mac.

1

Enable Developer Options

Tap Build Number 7 times in Settings → About Phone. One-time setup, takes 30 seconds.

2

Turn On USB Debugging

Enable USB Debugging in Developer Options. Plug in your cable or connect wirelessly with Android 11+.

3

Launch & Connect

Open AndroidFileSync — your device appears automatically. For WiFi, use the one-click pairing flow.

4

Start Managing

Browse files, drag & drop, manage apps, preview media. Everything works exactly like you expect on a Mac.

Install in seconds with Homebrew:

brew tap santosh7017/androidfilesync && brew install --cask androidfilesync
